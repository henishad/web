My dearest George,

Twenty-six. And now here you are. A man who paints the world more beautifully than most people see it. Who brings warmth into every room he walks into. Who loves with his whole chest and doesn't know how rare that is.

Knowing you has changed me. You have taught me what it looks like to care about things deeply, to pursue what moves you, and to be fully present. You always show up with gentleness, kindness, wit and laughter and you have made my world so much richer for it.

On this birthday, I hope you feel how loved you are. Not just by me but by everyone lucky enough to have you in their lives. You deserve every good thing. You deserve a year as beautiful as you are.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being mine. Happy birthday, pookies. 🐒🤍
