My dearest George,

Twenty-six. I keep turning that number over in my mind, thinking about all the years that shaped you into the person sitting across from me — the little boy in the Bob the Builder shirt who clearly believed he could fix anything, the tiny gentleman in the tuxedo who was already practicing for a life of making people smile, the kid with the bright eyes who had no idea yet how brightly he would shine.

And now here you are. A man who paints the world more beautifully than most people see it. Who brings warmth into every room he walks into. Who loves with his whole chest and doesn't know how rare that is.

I want you to know something: knowing you has changed me. You have taught me what it looks like to care about things deeply, to pursue what moves you, to be fully present. You show up with your whole self — your gentleness, your wit, your creativity — and you have made my world so much richer for it.

On this birthday, I hope you feel how loved you are. Not just by me — though you are, completely and without reservation — but by everyone lucky enough to have you in their lives. You deserve every good thing. You deserve a year as beautiful as the art you make.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being mine. Happy twenty-sixth birthday, George.

All my love, always — Henisha 🤍

With love, always.
